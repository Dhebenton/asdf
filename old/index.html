Common Questions

Machinery

How do I choose the right machine for my requirements?

Our team can help you compare machinery based on your workload, site conditions and budget, ensuring you find equipment suited to your requirements.

Can I view the machinery before making a purchase?

You can contact our team to arrange a viewing of available machinery at our location in Peetri, Estonia. Availability may vary between models.

Do you offer financing and equipment trade-in options?

Yes, we can assist with financing options and equipment trade-ins to help make your next machinery purchase more straightforward and manageable.

Servicing & Warranty

What types of machinery servicing and repairs do you provide?

We provide machinery servicing, fault diagnosis and repairs, alongside technical guidance to help maintain equipment performance and minimise unnecessary downtime.

Does purchased machinery come with warranty coverage?

Warranty coverage depends on the machine, manufacturer and purchase conditions. Our team can explain the applicable terms before you make a purchase.

Can you help source replacement parts for my equipment?

Our team can assist with identifying and sourcing suitable replacement parts for supported machinery brands, subject to availability and compatibility.

Our Team

Where is ETR located and which areas do you serve?

Our head office is located at Läike tee 1 in Peetri, Harju County. We supply machinery and support customers throughout Estonia.

How can I speak with someone about equipment enquiries?

You can reach our team through the contact page for assistance with machinery selection, product availability, financing and technical enquiries.

Can your team provide advice on machinery and attachments?

Yes, our specialists can help you understand equipment specifications, compare available models and identify suitable attachments for your intended applications.
